# Business Context

Aura Archives is a premium jewelry + clothing e-commerce store (prototype, no payments).
The differentiator is an editorial, museum-like browsing experience that feels refined,
warm, and human — never generic. Primary market is India (global reach), mobile-first
(60%+ mobile). Success: admin lists a product in < 2 min; a shopper finds and wishlists a
piece in < 30s; zero layout shift; Lighthouse 90+.
