# User Personas

- **The Curator-Shopper (25–45)** — design-literate, mobile, browses for editorial
  pieces; values trust signals (materials, authenticity) and a frictionless wishlist.
- **The Gift Buyer (30–60)** — less technical, wants reassurance and guidance; relies on
  the AI curator and clear sizing/shipping info.
- **The Young Browser (10–24)** — discovery-driven, mobile-only, low patience for
  clutter or slow pages.
- **The Admin (operator)** — needs to list a product in under two minutes, manage orders,
  and watch AI cost — all on desktop.
